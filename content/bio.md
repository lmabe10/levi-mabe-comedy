Levi Mabe is a stand-up comic on the rise, originally from Wichita, Kansas. His light-hearted, laid-back, clean-cut comedy has landed him on comedy club and theater stages all across the United States and Canada.

Levi has been featured on Nate Bargetze's "Nateland Live" and Dusty's Slay's "Grand Ole Comedy Show" in Nashville, TN at the legendary ZANIES Comedy Club. Levi is currently on an international tour with headliner Jeff Leeson.
